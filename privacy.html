晚安小夜灯 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 20 日

一句话总结：晚安小夜灯不收集、不上传、不分享任何个人数据。声音分析和梦话录音都只在你的手机上进行和保存。

我们收集哪些数据

不收集。App 没有账号系统，没有服务器，不包含任何广告、统计或第三方追踪代码，也不会联网发送任何信息。

麦克风

  • 声音检测（可在设置中关闭）：App 实时计算房间里声音的音量大小，用来判断孩子是否已经安静下来。这项功能只计算音量数值，不保存任何声音。
  • 梦话录制（默认关闭，需要家长在设置中主动打开）：检测到说话声时，App 会把这一小段声音录下来，保存在手机本地。
  • 录完后，App 会用 iOS 系统自带的声音分类功能，在手机上判断这段录音是说话、笑声、哭声还是其他声音。整个过程不联网。

录音存储在哪里

  • 录音只保存在这台手机的 App 目录里，并设置为不备份到 iCloud。
  • 录音会按家长设置的保留期限（3 天、7 天、30 天或一直保留）自动删除，家长也可以随时在“梦话记录”里手动删除。
  • 删除 App 时，所有录音会一并删除。
  • 只有在家长主动点“分享”时，录音才会通过系统分享功能发送到家长选择的地方。

闪光灯

App 只把背后的闪光灯当作补光灯使用，不会拍照或录像。

儿童隐私

这款 App 供家长为孩子设置使用。我们不会收集任何儿童（或成人）的个人信息。

本政策的变更

如果隐私政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

Goodnight Nightlight · Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

In short: Goodnight Nightlight does not collect, upload or share any personal data. Sound analysis and sleep-talk recordings stay on your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts and no servers. It contains no ads, analytics or third-party tracking, and it never sends information over the network.

Microphone

  • Sound detection (can be turned off): the app measures the loudness of the room in real time to tell whether the child has settled down. Only a loudness value is computed; no audio is saved.
  • Sleep-talk recording (off by default; a parent must turn it on): when speech is detected, the app saves a short clip locally on the device.
  • The clip is then classified on-device (talking, laughing, crying, other sounds) using Apple's built-in sound classifier. Nothing goes over the network.

Where recordings are stored

  • Recordings are stored only in the app's container on this iPhone and are excluded from iCloud backup.
  • They are deleted automatically after the retention period the parent chooses (3, 7 or 30 days, or kept), and can be deleted at any time.
  • Deleting the app deletes all recordings.
  • A recording leaves the device only if a parent taps Share and sends it somewhere.

Flashlight

The rear flashlight is used only as a light source. The camera is never used to take photos or video.

Children's privacy

The app is set up by parents for their children. We do not collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
